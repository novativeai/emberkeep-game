THE WORLD
High above the clouds, anchored to floating volcanic isles by chains of ancient gold, sits
Emberkeep — once the greatest dragon sanctuary ever built, now cold and quiet. Generations ago,
the Great Flame that warmed every nest flickered out, and the dragons curled into stone-like
slumber beneath blankets of ash. You arrive as the new Keeper, a young caretaker with one ember
in a lantern, and every tile you restore rekindles a little more of the sanctuary's heart.
THE TONE
Warm, hopeful, a little mischievous. This isn't a world of fearsome dragons — it's a world of sleepy,
clumsy, affectionate ones who hiccup sparks and hoard shiny pebbles like toddlers hoard toys.
THE EMOTIONAL ENGINE
Hatching. Every egg is a small promise; every hatchling has a personality the moment it opens its
eyes. The player isn't collecting items — they're raising a family.
THE CAST
The Keeper (the player's quiet presence), Cindra the elder fire-sprite who remembers the old
sanctuary and gives orders with grandmotherly bossiness, and Pip, a flightless messenger dragon
who delivers quests because he can't deliver himself anywhere by air.
THE ASSET UNIVERSE
Eggs in speckled, banded, and crystalline shells; hatchlings, juveniles, and elder dragons across
elemental families — ember, frost, storm, moss, and gold. Nests of woven branch and warm stone
that grow into grand brood-towers. Resource chains of flame gems, frost dew, and storm sparks.
Environments of ash-dusted isles that bloom into lava-veined gardens, hot springs, and bridges of
chained gold. Effects: drifting embers, hatching bursts, smoke-fog that lifts in glowing curls, tiny
flame sneezes.
THE PALETTE
Lava reds and marigold golds against deep plum stone, with cool teal skies for contrast — warmth
as the literal visual reward for progress.
