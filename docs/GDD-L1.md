## World & tone
Floating volcanic isles above the clouds; the Great Flame went out generations
ago and the sanctuary sleeps under ash. The player is the silent **Keeper**.
Warm, hopeful, a little mischievous. The emotional engine is **hatching** —
every egg is a small promise. Warmth is the visual reward: ash greys bloom into
reds/golds/moss as the player progresses.

## Cast (as shipped)
- **Eleanor**, of the **Daughters of the Moon** — the guide *and* the quest
  giver. She narrates all **21** tutorial steps, every post-tutorial hint
  (`dialogue.json → hints`), and owns the Ledger. Gold name-tag; animated
  bubble portrait from the disc atlas (`scripts/bake-portrait-disc.py`).
- **The Golden Elder** — silent for the entire game until she wakes at the
  Level-3 finale, where she speaks her only line. That withholding is the point:
  she is the sole living witness to what happened to the Great Flame.
- **Selyna** — Eleanor's counterpart in the order; art and talk banks ship, but
  she has no lines until the Borealis sanctuary.
- *Cindra and Laurah have been removed from the game entirely* — Eleanor took
  both roles. *Pip* was an earlier draft's tutorial voice and was never built.

## Board
An **authored 13×12 isometric grid with 46 playable tiles** (`src/data/map.json`,
GENERATED by `scripts/build-gamemap.mjs` from `world-map.json` — never
hand-edit). Tiles are skewed diamonds (420×242, ~30°) painted over baked
backdrops, with world-builder decor and an offscreen three.js crystal.
The board camera **frames one zone and glides to the next zone's focal cell on
level-up** (suppressed during the tutorial); UIScene has its own fixed camera.

### Regions (`map.json → regions`)
| Region | Unlock | Contents revealed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `level_1` | open at start | the starting zone (Theme Crystal at 8,11 — sheds Quartz) |
| `level_2_gate` | `{keys:1, level:2}` | 3 Cut Wood (the timber plot) |
| `level_2` | `{level:2}` | the Ancient Tree |
| `level_5` | `{level:3}` | the south terrace — opens at the cap (its demo-era `level:99` is gone) |

Ash-fog clouds cover every locked region; each fogged cell is authored (the
build script asserts it).

> **Note — the key is a one-shot tutorial prop.** The *only* key in the game is
> granted by the tutorial's `key_unlock` step (`effects: [{grantKeys: 1}]`), and
> it is spent immediately on `level_2_gate` — the lesson. In-fiction Eleanor calls
> it a **Stone Key**, not a Gold Key. After that the counter sits at 0 forever:
> every order and repeatable pays `keys: 0`, no chest gift or level-up grants
> one, and the shop deliberately sells none. All later regions are level-gated.
> That is coherent *for a demo*, but the Gold-Key economy MECHANICS §12 designs
> (chests, the Bronze→Silver→Gold merge ladder) is entirely Chapter Two work.

### Merge rules
- Default **3 adjacent → 1 next tier** (orthogonal flood-fill from the drop
  tile); **5 → 2** (`fiveBonus`).
- **Per-tier overrides** (`tier.merge`) change the rule for that tier's items:
  Red Dragon, Green Dragon and House all carry `{group: 2, outputs: 1}` — so
  **2 dragons → 1 Adult**, **2 Houses → 1 Manor**. An override disables the
  5-bonus for that tier.
- **Hatch** = a merge whose *output* tier equals the chain's `hatchAtTier`
  (3 for both dragon chains): 3 Eggs → a Dragon fires `item:hatched` with the
  shell-crack ceremony. `golden_egg.hatchAtTier` is 2, but the Golden Elder is
  awakened by the scripted finale, never by a player merge.

## Generators, Warmth & timers
- **Tap generators** (dragons): tap a ready one → spend **1 Warmth** → drop
  produce on a free tile within `REWARD_SPAWN_RADIUS` (3) → cooldown
  (Red/Green Dragon 45 s, Adults 30 s). They ALSO gift passively
  (180 s / 120 s) with no tap and no Warmth.
- **Passive generators** (`tappable:false` — House, Manor, Ancient Tree) and the
  free tap producers (Ripe Emberberry → an Emberberry, `energyCost: 0` / 20 s,
  and one Emberberry Sprout per **12** berries — its `generator.bonus`, the only
  way back to a second patch — Theme Crystal → a **Quartz Pebble** every
  300 s) drip on their own. **This is the "always something to do at zero
  Warmth" floor, and it is shipped** (MECHANICS §4.3).
- **Skip a timer** with **Gold** (the cheap path, ≤ `GENERATOR_SKIP_MAX_ENERGY`
  = 20, price ∝ fraction remaining) or **Warmth** (1.5× premium). Patience is
  always free.
- **Dragon Jobs**: send a dragon to a House and it works `DRAGON_WORK_MS` (3 min)
  advancing every timer at 2× per worker, then rests `DRAGON_REST_MS` (5 min).
- **Treasure Chest**: a permanent fixture that readies a gift every
  `CHEST_INTERVAL_MS` (5 min) — 15 Gold, 3 Emeralds, or 3 Rubies.
- **Warmth**: max **30** (+3 per Keeper level), start 28, **+1 / 60 s**,
  offline catch-up on load, banking up to `OFFLINE_BANK_CYCLES` (3) passive
  cycles for the "While you were away" card.

## Economy
**Gold** and **Warmth** are the live currencies. Gold comes from orders, Gold
Coins/Pouches (the House loop), and selling; it sinks into timer skips and
Warmth refills in the **Warmth Shop** (5/20/50 Warmth for 20/60/130 Gold, after
one free Ember Spark in the tutorial). The **Gold Shop** carries mock real-money
tags ($2.99/$9.99/$19.99) as an IAP showcase. **Keys are never sold** — they
gate story, not power.

## Orders & tasks
**Eleanor's Ledger**, `orders.json`:
1. *Light the Brazier* — 6× Gem Shard → 25 G, 30 XP, **and the Golden Egg
   appears on the Golden Altar** (the demo's MacGuffin).
2. *Warm the Long Hearth* — 3× Flame Gem → 75 G, 35 XP.
3. *Craft the Radiant Centerpiece* — 1× Radiant Gem → 110 G, **80 XP**. That XP
   is load-bearing, not a reward curve: it is what carries the scripted floor
   past `LEVEL_XP[2] = 220` so the Level-3 finale fires on THIS delivery for
   every player, rather than only for those who merged enough on the way (it was
   50, and the finale slipped to Order 4 for a player who merged nothing
   optional — see quest-ladder.md §4).
4. *Fill the Keeper's Hoard* — 3× Radiant Gem → 240 G, 85 XP.

Plus **4 repeatable orders** so the Ledger never dead-ends. A second Ledger tab
holds the **Keeper's Tasks** chapter checklist (`tasks.json`): hatch 4 dragons ·
complete 5 orders · earn 500 Gold · merge 30× · tap the Golden Elder 10 times
(locked until `eleanor_brazier` + Level 3) → **150 Gold + 5 Warmth**.

The top-right **quest tracker** does not read either directly: it reads the
**quest ladder** (`quests.json` → `QuestSystem`), which turns the four orders,
the Keeper's Tasks and the endless encore into six quests with ordered
subquests, so the HUD's sub-rows are always the steps *of* the line above them.
Every subquest is proved reachable at its own point on the ladder, offline, by
`pnpm quests` — see [quest-ladder.md](quest-ladder.md).

## The Emberkeep Cookbook
Every merge the player discovers is **inscribed as a recipe** (`CookbookPanel`,
`cookbook:discovered`) with a running discovered-count. Eleanor introduces it on
the tutorial's first merge (`cookbook_intro` / `cookbook_close`). It is the
shipped seed of the collection meta MECHANICS §14 calls for — a reason to merge
something you don't currently need.

## Progression & the finale
`LEVEL_XP = [0, 60, 220]`. The tutorial earns **exactly 60 XP** so Level 2 lands
on its scripted `levelup` beat; Level 3 (220) is the cap and opens `level_5`'s
land. **The array ends at 3 on purpose** — the chapter is complete, and the XP
bar reads "Chapter One complete" at the cap rather than filling toward nothing.
Every level-up pays a full Warmth refill + scaling Gold (`LEVELUP_REWARD`), Level
3 included — it is an ordinary level-up now.

**The finale fires on a QUEST**, `GOLDEN_ALTAR.awakenQuestId` (the Keeper's
Hoard), not on a level — she wakes one quest before `keepers_tasks` asks the
player to commune with her. It is one shared timeline (`FINALE` in Constants.ts),
so BoardScene and UIScene stay in step without cross-scene calls:
camera glides west to the **Golden Altar** → the **Golden Egg cracks and the
Golden Elder awakens** (900 / 2000 ms) → **the Golden Elder speaks for the first
time in the game** — *"the Great Flame didn't die — it was taken"* → camera
home. Play continues straight from there, with the Elder standing at her altar
for communing taps.

The demo's teaser tail is GONE (full production is being built instead): the fly
to the south terrace, the fog parting on Chapter-Two silhouettes, the "Beyond the
demo" roadmap and the Chapter One card with its Keep Playing / Play Again
buttons. `level_5`'s ash-clouds now lift at the cap instead of standing forever.

## Session profile
~25 minutes to the finale in one sitting, or 2–3 cozy sessions with natural stop
points after Orders 1 and 2 (each ends on a celebration). Rule enforced
throughout: **a peak at least every 3 minutes** — a hatch, an order, an unlock,
a chest, a bloom.

## Out of scope for the demo
Star Shards / premium currency, festivals, dailies & login streaks, Flame
Shrines, cascade merges, bubbles, per-tile rekindle, Sanctuary Warmth, dragon
stamina deep-naps, additional isles beyond the south terrace, login,
localization, pinch zoom (pointer taps/drags work for touch).
