# Naming — the kid-clarity pass (target: 8–13)

> **Status: SPEC — the authoritative old→new map.** The lore does not change;
> the words that carry it do. Every rename below keeps the thing's meaning,
> silhouette and place in the story, and swaps only the label. **Ids never
> change** — saves, `q:done:` latches, `GOLDEN_ALTAR.awakenQuestId` and
> `lockedUntil` gates all key on ids, so this pass touches display strings
> only. Wiring scope is §8.
>
> The test for every name and line in this file: **a 10-year-old reads it once
> and knows what to do, what the thing is, and what just happened in the
> story.**

---

## 1. The five naming laws

1. **A quest title is a verb plus a thing you can see.** "Light the Fire Bowl",
   not "What She Keeps". If the title is a riddle, the riddle goes in the
   blurb and the task goes in the title.
2. **A task row is one action: verb + number + item.** "Make 2 Berry Jams."
   "Give Eleanor 1 Berry Jam." Never two actions in one row.
3. **A chain's tiers share a family word** (T1/T2 at minimum), so a kid sees
   what merges into what: Moss Puff → Moss Bundle → Moss Bale. The T3 may
   transform into a "wow" name (Dew Drop → Dew Vial → **Moonwater**) — that
   transformation IS the reward.
4. **No word a 10-year-old stumbles on reading aloud.** Banned on sight:
   brazier, larder, hearth, hoard, orrery, lodestone, billet, spile, dram,
   cordial, nodule, cairn, wrack, helm, kiln, bolt (of cloth), moulted.
   A hard word survives only if the art teaches it on screen (Bellows,
   Spindle, Rune).
5. **The depth stays in the meaning, never in the vocabulary.** "Names are the
   one thing nobody thought to take" is deep AND simple — that is the bar.
   The eight proper nouns all pass and all stay: *Emberkeep · Borealis · the
   Moonhold · Daughters of the Moon · the Great Flame · the Keeping · the
   Lantern · Silas.*

## 7. Dialogue — the register rules, and the wired lines rewritten

Rules for every line, current and future:

1. **≤2 sentences, one idea per bubble**; target ≤120 chars (the 180 budget is
   a wall, not a goal).
2. **No idioms, no inverted irony.** A kid reads "I've decided not to look a
   gift Keeper in the mouth" literally. Eleanor's dryness survives as
   *understatement*, never as vocabulary.
3. **Each reveal is stated once in words a kid can retell.** "The dragons
   didn't die. They fell asleep. Sleeping things can be woken." Canon already
   does this at its best — hold the whole script to its best.
4. **Keep Eleanor's tic** — it is kid-legible: *"I was— never mind. Later."*
5. **The Elder keeps her capitals and grandeur** — in shorter sentences.
6. **Never touched:** *"the Great Flame didn't die — it was taken."* Already
   perfect for a 10-year-old, and load-bearing canon.

**Chapter 2 beats** (wired, `dialogue.json → chapters.2`) — rewritten:

> That's the first thing anyone has given back to this place in sixty years. I told myself I wouldn't cry about it. Well. Too late.

> You should know what my magic does. I can catch a thing, hold it, and give it back. That's all of it.

> The moon does the same trick every night. It borrows light and gives it back, softer. Nobody calls that magic. But it is.

> So when I ask you for moonwater, remember — I never make anything new. Everything I do, someone else lit first.

**Ledger banter** (wired, banks 1–6) — rewritten:

- **I** · "Done already? I won't ask how. I'm just glad you're here." · "Warmer. Not warm yet. But warmer counts." · "That's today's work. Rest — the list will still be here tomorrow." · "Good. Do that again tomorrow and I'll start to believe it's real."
- **II** · "Something up the hill just lifted its head. It felt what you did." · "Another one done. The island is keeping count, even if you aren't." · "You work like someone who's done this before. You haven't — I checked." ✓
- **III** · "Thank you. I mean it. I don't have many easy words today, so take that one." · "Take the gold and buy something silly with it. That's an order." · "It's going faster than it should. I'm not complaining. It just scares me a little."
- **IV** · "Done. And you're still here. I notice that every single time." · "I keep thinking I'll look up and you'll be gone. Ignore me. The next job is on the shelf." · "You're better at this than I ever was. That's not me being kind. I've counted."
- **V** · "She watches you work, you know. She doesn't watch me." ✓ · "Whatever you think of me now — you're still here, still helping. I've stopped trying to work out why." · "Thank you, Keeper. I've started being able to say the word." ✓
- **VI** · "My sister will say we're doing this the slow way. She says that about everything." · "Cold work up here. Her magic holds things; mine gives them back. I'm learning, and I like it. That's new."

## 8. Wiring scope (display strings only — ids frozen)

- `quests.json` + `orders.json` — titles move TOGETHER (same-name law) and
  subquest labels take §2–4's task lines.
- `chains.json` (or wherever tier display names live) — §6, both worlds.
- `dialogue.json` — §7's ch-2 beats and six banter banks; keep the stored
  format (curly quotes + attribution).
- `tutorial.json` — sweep for renamed words (Brazier, Preserve, Larder…).
- `dragondex.json` — breed/legendary display names (Ash Dragon, Ice Dragon).
- `tasks.json` — task labels to law-2 grammar.
- **e2e**: `tests/e2e/level1.spec.ts` may assert display text — update
  assertions with the strings, in the same change.
- Docs follow the map, not the other way round: after wiring, sweep
  quest-ladder.md, merge-chains.md, GDD-L1.md, story-map.md tables for the new
  names, then run story-qa's `coherence.py`.
