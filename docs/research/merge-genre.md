# Merge genre research (source notes for MECHANICS.md)

Deep study of the top merge titles — **Merge Dragons!, Merge Mansion, Travel
Town, Gossip Harbor, EverMerge, Love & Pies** — done 2026-06-13 to ground
[MECHANICS.md](../MECHANICS.md). Numbers are "accurate as of the cited period";
re-verify against the live game before hard-coding. Primary high-confidence
sources: Deconstructor of Fun teardowns, Naavik deep-dives, official help
centres; community wikis corroborate but drift with patches.

## A. Core merge mechanics
- **Two schools.** *Merge-3 with a 5→2 bonus* (Merge Dragons, EverMerge, Merge
  Magic): 3 alike → 1; 5 → 2; 10 → 3. The 5-merge's ~20% bonus is the genre's
  optimisation/"mastery" hook. *Merge-2* (Merge Mansion, Travel Town, Gossip
  Harbor, Love & Pies): 2 → 1, no bonus; simpler, pairs with order boards.
- **Chains 7–15 tiers**, several interlocking (nestled/combined) chains live at
  once. Top-tier costs are astronomical & obfuscated (Gossip Harbor bread tier
  15 ≈ 16,000 base items).
- **Max-tier "Wonders"** can't merge further → become **idle producers** (loot
  on creation, then rewards every ~10 h active). Completion ≠ dead end.
- **Harvest merges:** most items are tappable producers; **dragons have stamina**
  ("Too Tired" gate). **Land restoration** (Merge Dragons Dead Land) / **fog
  clearing** (EverMerge wands) fuses merging to map expansion. **Cascade combos**
  (a small merge triggers a chain reaction) are the biggest dopamine spike.
- **Board-full is a designed feature:** fixed ~63-tile single screen that returns
  20–25% empty; **sell**, paid storage, and **bubbling** (off-grid overflow) as
  valves.

## B. Energy / waiting
- Genre default: **cap 100, +1 / 2 min, full refill on level-up, offline regen,
  pauses at cap.** Merge Mansion 100 / 2 min; Travel Town 100 / ~90 s, cap rises
  with level; Gossip Harbor tiered action costs 1/2/4.
- **Merge Dragons has NO energy bar** — paced by Healing Power + timed chests.
  Proof the bar is optional.
- **Dual gating:** energy (shared session pool) + **per-producer cooldowns**
  (Travel Town's 13 auto-producers run free & offline). Always something to do
  at zero energy.
- **Timed chests:** 1–30 min, instant-open for gems or **merge keys** (2 keys);
  merging a chest resets its expiry. Skill substitutes for waiting/paying.
- **Cozy recommendation:** forgiving or no energy bar, full refill on level-up,
  free offline auto-producers, short skill-skippable timers, overflow never
  wasted, opt-in gentle notifications, ~3–6 short sessions/day with closure.

## C. Progression & XP
- Player level is real in most (Merge Mansion cap **50**, soft/raised by updates;
  Gossip Harbor reaches ~10 in 2 sessions). Love & Pies has **no XP** — runs on
  task/chapter gating.
- **Orders are the primary XP source; only high-tier/terminal merges grant XP
  directly** (Merge Mansion: only L5+ merges spawn XP stars). Intermediate
  merges ≈ 0. No combo-score in narrative mergers.
- **Level-up = multi-reward burst anchored by a full energy refill** (Travel
  Town, Gossip Harbor, Love & Pies); Merge Mansion adds alternating brown/blue
  chests by odd/even level.
- Leveling **gates capability**: Gossip Harbor 2× booster @15, 4× @40; new
  chains/areas/energy-cap by level.
- **Secondary meta:** Merge Dragons "Dragon Power" (1–15,000) gates events/dens;
  Merge Mansion 828-item Album rewards per completed chain. End-game = events +
  collection, not a leveling finale.

## D. Orders, currencies, keys, events
- **Order boards:** 3–6 persistent character requests ("deliver N of item X") +
  ~1 auto-order; orders **wait, never fail**; self-correcting difficulty; long
  deferred **project orders** (Gossip Harbor decoration quest ≈ 512 items / 43
  days).
- **Currencies:** soft (coins — story/renovation only, decoupled from power) +
  premium (gems — energy refills, timer skips, storage, bubble pops; escalating
  daily price) + energy + temporary event currency. **Keys:** area/unlock tokens
  (Merge Mansion Golden Key = pass; EverMerge wands) AND a bronze→silver→gold
  **merge mini-chain** (2 keys open a chest; 1 silver ≈ 2.5 bronze; gold only by
  merging silver).
- **Unlock spiral:** areas gate on level, level comes from completing that area's
  tasks (Merge Mansion 24 areas). Fog/void = the metered spatial reward.
- **Events:** single-player sawtooth races (3 milestones), board/dice metas,
  short competitive tournaments; **28–30 day battle pass** (free + premium, no
  tier-skip) as the retention spine; **daily tasks** (Merge Mansion 6/day → gem
  box) + **7-day login streak**.
- **Meta-spine = renovation:** spend soft currency to rebuild a place (mansion /
  hotel / café); each rebuild unlocks a **story beat + a generator + a small
  payout**; 2–3 free cosmetic choices; one **central deflected mystery** sustains
  the long arc.
